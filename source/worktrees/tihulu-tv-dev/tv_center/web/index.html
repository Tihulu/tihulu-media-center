Languages and engines

The native app offers DE / EN / TR, installed Argos translation packages and a user-installed multilingual Whisper model. No model is downloaded by this page.

Captions use real Teletext first when configured; ASR can be selected or used as a fallback. Live translated text may lag behind the broadcast.
